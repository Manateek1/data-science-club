Sponsor Acalanes Data Fest

A free, student-run data science and AI hackathon for up to 50 Bay Area high school students, organized by Acalanes Data Science Club and hosted by BRIDGEGOOD.

9 AM–5 PM Pacific
Where
BRIDGEGOOD studio

The event

Students work in teams of 1–4 to explore real datasets, build projects, and present to judges. Beginners are welcome. Projects may cover community impact, finance, machine learning, and data visualization.

The top three teams are invited to showcase their work at BRIDGEGOOD’s Inspiration Awards on December 11, 2026, at Salesforce Tower in San Francisco, connecting with tech professionals and industry leaders.

What sponsors receive

  • Acalanes Data Fest sponsorship, including sponsor mentions at the November 21, 2026 hackathon.
  • A full year of club sponsorship after Data Fest, with mentions at every Acalanes Data Science Club event until the sponsorship ends on November 21, 2027.
  • Frequent sponsor mentions during the club’s weekly meetings at Acalanes High School.

How to contribute

Contribute funding, food, drinks, prizes, or project materials to help keep the event free. You can also ask about mentoring or apply to judge.
