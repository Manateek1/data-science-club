Before you join

Questions, answered.

Who can participate, and how many spots are there?

Acalanes Data Fest is for Bay Area high school students. Registration is free and capped at 50 participants.

What will teams work on?

Teams explore a real dataset, choose a question, and build a data science or AI project. Topics may include finance, machine learning, community impact, and data visualization. There is one overall competition.

Do I need coding or data science experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Bring curiosity, ask questions, and use the support of your teammates, mentors, and peers.

Can I come without a team?

Yes. You can register with a team or find teammates at the event. Teams may have one to four students.

What is the schedule for November 21?

The event runs from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM at BRIDGEGOOD’s Oakland studio. The planned day includes a kickoff, team project time, lunch, five-minute demos to judges, and a closing. See the day plan for the current schedule.

What can teams win?

There is more than $2,000 in prizes. CodeCrafters VIP memberships are provided for each member of the top three teams: two years for first place, one year for second, and six months for third. Every team can also use ArgosX security scans, with extra scan prizes for the top three.

What happens after the top teams are announced?

The top three teams are invited to BRIDGEGOOD’s Inspiration Awards on December 11 at Salesforce Tower in San Francisco. BRIDGEGOOD covers their tickets and prints each team’s 24 × 36-inch project poster for the showcase.

Where is the event?

BRIDGEGOOD Studio, 95 Washington St, Oakland, CA 94607. See BRIDGEGOOD’s directions and parking information.

How do I register?

Reserve your free spot through Luma.
