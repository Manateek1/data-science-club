# Acalanes Data Fest sponsor prospects and drafts

This is the active workflow for sponsor outreach. Run it manually in this chat when Dillon asks. There are no recurring sponsor-scout runs. The old `Code.gs` Apps Script is retained as a legacy reference; do not install it or use it to create batches.

## On each request

1. Read the live **Acalanes Data Fest Sponsor Outreach** spreadsheet before researching. Treat its **Prospects**, **Suppressions**, and **Responses** tabs as the current record.
2. Research relevant prospects using official company pages and published business contact routes. Confirm that each organization is a reasonable fit and use only contact details shown by a reliable source. Do not require the prospect to have previously requested information before researching them or preparing an unsent draft.
3. Before drafting, compare each organization, email address, and company domain against the spreadsheet and Gmail **Drafts**, **Scheduled**, and **Sent**, plus relevant replies. If a matching draft or message already exists, update its tracker row and do not create another. Do not contact a suppressed organization or an address that has opted out. For contact-form-only organizations, record the form and skip the email draft.
4. Add each new prospect to **Prospects** with its published contact route, source, current status, date, and Gmail item ID. Record `Draft saved`, `Scheduled`, `Sent`, `Bounced`, `Reply received`, or `Form only` only when that state is verified. Update an existing row when its state changes.
5. Create unsent Gmail drafts only. Do not send, schedule, follow up, or run this process automatically. Dillon will ask in this chat when he wants another batch.

## Draft style

- Sound natural, friendly, and like a high school student. Keep the wording direct and not corporate. Vary the opening; do not start every email with "I'm Dillon."
- Be very thankful and appreciative.
- Say that cash sponsorship is most helpful for food, prizes, and event costs. Make clear that useful in-kind support, such as food, drinks, prizes, software, compute, or project materials, is also appreciated.
- Keep the sponsorship ask informative but general. Do not name specific tiers or amounts.
- Do not attach the packet or include links.
- Invite them to reply for the prospectus, which explains sponsorship options and recognition, or for other helpful information.
- Personalize only with details verified from the prospect's official sources. Keep event facts accurate: Acalanes Data Fest is a free, student-run data science and AI event for 50 Bay Area high school students, hosted at BRIDGEGOOD in Oakland on November 21, 2026. Do not say "exactly 50" or "up to 50."
- Use the established Data Science Leadership sender when available. Leave CC and BCC blank unless Dillon asks or the existing email chain already includes those recipients.
- End every outreach email exactly with:
  ```
  Best,
  Dillon
  Acalanes Data Science Club
  ```

## Tracker notes

The **Prospects** tab is the duplicate-prevention source of truth. Match organization names, email addresses, and business domains; check Gmail as well because scheduled or sent messages may not be reflected in the sheet yet. Record source pages in the tracker, not in the draft body. Historical notes in the notes column are context, not eligibility gates.

Keep opt-outs and bounced addresses in **Suppressions**. Record replies and outcomes in **Responses**. Never invent an address, contact, personalization detail, or delivery status.
